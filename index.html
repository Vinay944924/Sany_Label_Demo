Where this connects to stage one

The Augmentir shift handover I rebuilt in stage one already has an allergen changeover check, and a rule that a Fail forces a note and a QA signature to appear. That rule and this label engine are the same idea at two scales: paper can only ask; software can insist.

The missing link is that the handover's allergen check and this pack label are not yet reading the same object. Make Gensis the parent of both and the changeover check knows what the batch declares before the operator does — instead of asking a tired night-shift lead to remember.

The finding worth acting on. Every panel on b-resilience.com is in EU format — kilojoules, per 100 g, Polyols, Salt. A bar sold from Jonesboro needs an FDA Nutrition Facts panel, and three of its mandatory fields are not captured anywhere in the current data: Added Sugars, Trans Fat, and the four micronutrients. The US label cannot be generated from what SANY holds today. That is a spec problem, not a design problem, and it is cheaper to fix before the shop exists than after.
